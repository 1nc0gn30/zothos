     The best ideas come from personal frustration or boring, overlooked industries. I was surprised about how many of these founders  build "boring" businesses. These markets have fewer competitors, stronger demand, and real buyers with urgent needs.

     Building is easy now. Anyone can ship a product in a weekend.
