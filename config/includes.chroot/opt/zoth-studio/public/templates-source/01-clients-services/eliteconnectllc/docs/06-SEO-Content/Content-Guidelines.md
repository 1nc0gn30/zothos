# Content Guidelines

## Tone

- Professional but approachable
- Local-first (Virginia Beach, Hampton Roads)
- Specific over generic
- Active voice
- Short sentences

## What to Avoid

- ❌ "We are the leading provider..."
- ❌ "Cutting-edge solutions..."
- ❌ "Leverage our expertise..."
- ❌ Generic SaaS boilerplate
- ❌ Placeholder text left in production

## What to Use Instead

- ✅ "Elite Connect plans commercial security as one system."
- ✅ "The same team that installs it supports it."
- ✅ "No cable spaghetti behind. Every run is planned, labeled, and photographed."
- ✅ "Free consultation. Local team. No pressure."

## Service Descriptions

Each service should answer:
1. What is it?
2. Who needs it?
3. How does Elite Connect do it differently?

## FAQ Style

- Question in plain language
- Answer in 1-2 sentences
- Include specific details (license numbers, service areas)
- Avoid "it depends" without context

## Headline Formula

Eyebrow (brand blue, uppercase) → H2 (bold, specific) → Body (1-2 sentences, muted color)

Example:
> **WHAT WE DO**
> ## Nine services. One team. No finger-pointing.
> Every system is designed to work with the others.
