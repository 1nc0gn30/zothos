## Offer Decisions

- The agreed launch system is not just a website. The site must drive newsletter signups, sell the paid community, show the event calendar, and make it easy to book with the team.
- The paid front door is a one-time community/library purchase near $197/$200 for templates, skills, prompts, and special access.
- The free acquisition path is a weekly masterclass and event calendar, with Cityblock/podcast guest network activation and LinkedIn as important channels.
- The retained service is the Healthcare Software Factory path: analyze practice data and workflows, scope back-office work, build or customize the MVP, and create a three-month plan to digitalize systems.
- The core buyer from the latest differentiated messaging is a solo or small private-practice physician owner.
- The strongest pains are after-hours documentation burden, staffing costs, and AI implementation friction.
- The customer outcomes are getting home earlier, giving patients more attention, avoiding unnecessary hires, reducing admin work, and getting one trusted partner instead of juggling tools.
- "Healthcare Engineer" is a deliberate pattern interrupt and should remain prominent.
