# Compounding Intelligence System — Scalable Sales System

Date: 2026-06-13
Framework: Work-Bench customer-acquisition system, adapted from the Chai With Jai paid-discovery model.

## The Core Insight: The Demo Is The Close

Most consultants give away free discovery calls and strategy sessions — the bootcamp playbook. It attracts tire-kickers and devalues the time. We do the opposite.

The working session — where we connect the prospect's real data and surface something economically valuable in the room — **is** the product. The $500 setup is not a leap of faith; by the time we name the price, they've already watched their own data produce a callable customer list. The demo qualifies and closes in one motion.

> Imtiaz moved from "what does your pricing look like" to booking a same-day follow-up — because he'd already seen 25 of his best customers surfaced with phone numbers. The artifact closed him.

## The Funnel

| Stage | What Happens | Price | Conversion Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Warm signal** | A workshop attendee, referral, or someone who saw the work asks how to do it. | Free | — |
| **Working session** (60-75 min) | Structure → connect their data → one non-obvious insight → one economically valuable artifact → kick off one always-on job. Discovery runs throughout. | The $500 setup *is* this, delivered — or a paid diagnostic that credits toward setup. | 40%+ to retainer |
| **Retainer** | $85/hr ongoing; hour blocks or project scope. | $85/hr | — |
| **Referral** | Delivered client refers 10-20/mo (Imtiaz's projection). | — | The growth engine |

Decision: don't give away the diagnostic for free. Either it *is* the paid $500 setup (deliver value, get paid), or it's a paid diagnostic that credits toward the setup. You only spend deep time on people who put money down — which filters tire-kickers and lifts conversion.

### Why Workshops Are The #1 Channel
The studio already has gravity: mentors (Vogue-shot photographers, hot97 showrunners, filmmakers with a streaming app), a screening room, a podcast room, a Thursday community showcase. Prospects who watch Jai connect data and surface a callable list at a workshop are pre-qualified, high-intent buyers who've experienced a micro-version of the product. The working session is the natural next step — not "let me tell you about my offer," but "I noticed your questions; want me to do this on *your* data?"

## Step 4 — The Working Session (qualify + close)

Run `discovery-and-demo-playbook.md`. The session *is* the sale because it delivers real value (a callable list, a cash truth, a content engine) regardless of whether they buy more. Name the price after the artifact lands. Anchor: $2,000+/mo OBM vs. $200/mo plan + known retainer.

## Step 5 — Compound

- Every delivery becomes a **case study** (Imtiaz = `../../meanwhile-back-in-queens/`) and a reusable demo asset.
- Every client becomes a **referral node**.
- Every context doc/skill/automation built makes delivery faster next time → margin improves as the library grows.
- The best pool prospects get **elevated to featured case studies**, which become the proof for the next cohort.

## The Repeatable Loop

```
Workshop / referral  →  Working session (demo = close)  →  $500 setup delivered
        ↑                                                          ↓
   Referral node  ←  Case study + retainer  ←  Compounding library + seats
```

## Scaling Past Solo

- **Multi-seat delivery:** train Tamid-style operators / interns on a second seat to run hand-off jobs and eventually deliver setups.
- **Productize the demo kit:** the 6-part demo asset kit (`discovery-and-demo-playbook.md`) makes the working session repeatable by someone other than Jai.
- **Workshop → cohort:** turn the 101 workshop into a recurring funnel that feeds the MMO pool every month.
