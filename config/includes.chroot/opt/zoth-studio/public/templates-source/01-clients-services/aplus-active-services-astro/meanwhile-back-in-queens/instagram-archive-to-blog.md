# Instagram Archive → Owned Blog (Content Repository)

Date: 2026-06-13

## The Problem It Solves

The brand's most valuable asset — research-grade storytelling about Queens (Dutch coffee history, architecture, Jamaica, the diaspora) — is trapped on Instagram. It is not searchable, not citable, and not owned. People already cite this content in research papers, but only because they found it on Instagram. On an owned, searchable site it becomes a primary source and a top-of-funnel engine.

> "We've been doing it a disservice because it only lives on Instagram where it could be searchable on our website."

## The Job (goal mode, running)

A long-running Codex goal-mode job:
1. Read through the ~849 Instagram posts.
2. Convert posts (including slide carousels and captions) into CMS-ready content.
3. Prototype the blog.
4. Persistent memory includes the key context: **people cite this content in research papers** — so structure it to be citable and AEO-friendly.

Status: running; produced an early blog shape with real images after a steering fix (signed image URLs were not durable for the prototype — download the signed image, then proceed).

## Steering Required (don't let it run blind)

The model will exit jobs early and make mediocre choices. Steering moves that work:
- **Curate to a season:** "Out of the 849 posts, pick 10 we want to turn into a blog first." Then expand.
- **Enrich, don't just migrate:** pull per-post insight — comments, slide content, what resonated — because that engagement *is* the basis of the brand.
- **Tie to Substack:** package the best ~20 as the newsletter's season one (`substack-newsletter-strategy.md`).
- **Provide a creative brief** as the steering artifact for tone and selection.

## Why This Is A Clean Hand-off Job

- Well-scoped: known input (IG archive) → known output (CMS-ready blog).
- Perfect for **seat 2** (Tamid / an intern): meaningful work that does not require Imtiaz's creative brain on every step.
- Runs continuously and improves itself; Imtiaz reviews and steers, then hands off.

## Strategic Payoff

- **SEO + AI-engine optimization:** own the "#1 source on Queens" position so search and AI assistants cite the brand.
- **Owned, durable asset:** content lives on the brand's property, not a platform that can change retention or reach (note: IG business-analytics retention is only 90 days, so historical insight must be captured to the owned store).
- **Feeds the funnel:** research citations → authority → discovery → drop demand.

## Caveats

- IG business dashboard data retention is ~90 days; export what matters now.
- Image rights / signed-URL durability needs handling per the steering note above.
- Expect to throw away a chunk of the auto-generated structure; the value is in curation.
