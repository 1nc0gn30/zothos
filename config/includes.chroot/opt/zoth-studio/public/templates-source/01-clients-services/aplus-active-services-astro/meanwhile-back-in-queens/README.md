# Meanwhile Back in Queens (Queens & Made)

Featured case study, elevated from the MMO pool on 2026-06-13.

> Imtiaz / Queens & Made is the first live delivery of the Compounding Intelligence System offer. This folder is both the client case study and the proof artifact that hardens the productized offer in `../offers/compounding-intelligence-system/`.

## Who This Is

- **Founder:** Imtiaz (`ntiaz@queensandmade.com`). Cousin **Tamid** (Jersey City) is starting to operate alongside him.
- **Brand:** *Meanwhile Back in Queens* — a faceless lifestyle brand rooted in Jamaica, Queens.
- **Model:** Drop-based e-commerce + documentary-grade content. ~$500K/year in drops, targeting the $1M mark this year.
- **Stack:** Shopify (store, bank, and credit card all integrated; Mercury for the Mets payout), QuickBooks (no service revenue last 2 years), Shopify email, "Community" SMS list, Instagram (~849 posts).
- **Operating constraint:** ADHD / executive dysfunction. Admin, accounting, lines of credit, and email follow-up fall through the cracks. He wants to spend his brain on creative direction and campaign storytelling, and hand the rest to a system.

## What We Agreed (the offer he bought)

- **$500 flat** to connect email, calendar, documents, and key business systems into one compounding intelligence system on Codex.
- **$85/hour** retainer for ongoing work, with the option to pre-buy blocks (e.g. 10 hours) or scope a specific project (e.g. drop pre-work).
- Imtiaz believes he can refer **10-20 people/month** into the same offer.
