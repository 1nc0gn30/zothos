# Compounding Intelligence System — ROI Drivers

Date: 2026-06-13

The drivers to tease in every conversation. Frame everything as TIME, COST, or RISK — the buyer's business ROI of solving vs. not solving. Unlike the merch buyer (whose ROI is emotional), this buyer's ROI is operational and financial, so quantify it.

## The Three-Lens Tease (use live)

| Lens | The Tease | The Proof Move |
|---|---|---|
| **TIME** | "You're spending your highest-value hours on the work your brain is worst at." | Show a follow-up draft generated in seconds; a report that runs on a schedule. |
| **COST** | "An online business manager is $2,000+/mo. This plan is $200/mo — and it just found customers you didn't know you had." | Surface the p99 callable list (names + phones) in the room. |
| **RISK** | "You're making drop and cash decisions on gut. Let's ground them in your actual statements." | Pull the real revenue time series; spot the spikes and the leakage. |

## Quantifying The Tease (ranges to use, then ground in their data)

- **TIME:** "If admin + follow-up + reporting is ~8-12 hrs/week, and your creative hours are worth far more than $85, the math closes itself."
- **COST:** "$2,000+/mo OBM vs. $200/mo plan + a known retainer. Plus whatever the p99 list is worth once you call it."
- **RISK:** "With ~6 months runway and lines of credit out, the cost of one gut-wrong drop decision dwarfs the setup fee."

Always replace the ranges with *their* numbers from *their* data in the room. The buyer trusts their data, not our claims.

## The Compounding ROI (why the retainer is cheap)

Every context doc, skill, and automation:
- makes the whole organization faster, not just the founder;
- transfers to the next teammate on a new seat;
- turns one-off insight into a recurring, self-improving job.

So the ROI curve bends up over time: the $500 setup is the wedge, and each retainer hour buys an asset that keeps paying. Frame the retainer as buying *compounding assets*, not *time*.
