# MMO Pool — Registry & Elevation Mechanism

Date: 2026-06-13

## What The MMO Pool Is

The MMO pool is the standing pool of warm prospects sourced from the workshop series (`../../workshops/`) and the studio's gravity — the monetization-with-AI opportunity pool. Pool members are tracked lightly; the best are **elevated to featured case studies** with their own top-level project folder, the way `../../meanwhile-back-in-queens/` and `../../stg-kvibe-pod/` are.

> Assumption flagged: "MMO" is taken here as the workshop's monetization/AI opportunity pool. If it stands for something specific (a named cohort, "Make Money with AI", etc.), rename this file and the label — the mechanism stays the same.

## The Elevation Mechanism (same as stg-kvibe-pod)

```
Workshop attendee / referral
        ↓  (warm signal)
   MMO pool (tracked in the workshop CRM)
        ↓  (working session delivered + offer fit)
   Elevated → featured case study (own folder)
        ↓
   Proof asset + referral node for the next cohort
```

A prospect earns elevation when: (1) a working session was delivered, (2) there's a real offer fit, and (3) the engagement produces reusable proof (a case study + demo assets). Elevation is not about deal size; it's about whether the engagement hardens an offer.
