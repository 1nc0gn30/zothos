# Compounding Intelligence System — The Offer

The productized offer Jai sells out of the MMO pool. Imtiaz / Meanwhile Back in Queens is the first live delivery and the featured case study (`../../meanwhile-back-in-queens/`).

> Connect your email, calendar, documents, and key business systems into one compounding intelligence system on Codex — so the work you're worst at runs itself, and your time goes to what you're best at.

## The Package

- **$500 flat** — system integration: connect email, calendar, documents + key business systems, set up the harness, load grounding context, stand up first agents/automations.
- **$85/hour** — retainer for ongoing work. Pre-buyable in blocks (e.g. 10 hours) or scoped to a specific project (e.g. drop pre-work).

## The One-Line Thesis

The demo is the close. We don't sell a system; we connect the prospect's real data live, surface something economically valuable in the room (a callable high-value customer list, a cash-flow truth, a content engine), and the artifact closes the deal. Discovery makes the demo land; the retainer makes it compound.
