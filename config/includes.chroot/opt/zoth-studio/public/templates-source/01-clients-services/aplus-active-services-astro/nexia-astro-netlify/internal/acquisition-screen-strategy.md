## Strategy Synthesis

- The site should sell a Healthcare Software Factory retainer, not only a generic software factory.
- Slack alignment says the business system has four first-order jobs: sell Healthcare Hackathon Heroes, grow the Substack audience, show the event calendar, and make it easy to book with the team.
- The offer ladder is clear: $197 one-time access, free weekly masterclasses and office hours, then a $3K+/quarter Healthcare Software Factory for the teams that need retained help.
- Khizar validated the differentiated messaging and reinforced `Healthcare Engineer` as the pattern-interrupt category; the site should make that term feel owned, practical, and repeatable.
- The lean business model canvas throughline is: audience = physicians, clinicians, operators, podcast guests, and healthcare-adjacent founders; problem = admin drag, workflow confusion, AI implementation friction, and trust/compliance risk; solution = templates, classes, audits, demos, and retained build support; channels = LinkedIn, YouTube, Slack, Substack, Twitter/X, Cityblock/network activation, and podcast guests; revenue = access pass, retainer, and bespoke consulting.
- The first win is compound intelligence: use docs, email, calendar, events, demos, and office hours to make useful artifacts faster.
- Natural intelligence is the steering layer: brain workout classes and strategy games help people refine judgment before they automate.
- The scalable acquisition system is content of worked examples, office hours, demos, weekly events, and live/recorded distribution.
- The flywheel should name the channels clearly: LinkedIn live/streaming first, then YouTube, Slack, Substack, and Twitter/X as reusable distribution surfaces.
- Discovery should be diagnostic: needs -> pain -> impact -> urgency, then a small demo or ROI conversation.
- ROI language should focus on time returned, admin drag reduced, discovery-to-demo cycle time, fewer wrong-fit builds, and faster path to a retained plan.

## Screen Design Requirements

- Every nested screen needs a specific job in the funnel instead of a generic three-card filler section.
- Checkout and offer pages should separate DIY, DWY, and DFY paths so buyers choose by current state, not by package name.
- Top-level navigation should expose the retainer path as `Healthcare Software Factory`.
- The offer menu should make the retained menu concrete: workflow audit, compound intelligence system, natural intelligence class/game design, middle-office automation, back-office automation, Podcast Guest Launch, managed LinkedIn, and bespoke consulting.
- Resources and field-note pages should explicitly explain the acquisition flywheel.
- Masterclass pages should connect means, motivation, opportunity to the first small win.
- Proof/case-study pages should frame demos as worked examples that lead into discovery.
