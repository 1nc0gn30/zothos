# Compounding Intelligence System — Offer Definition

Date: 2026-06-13

## What It Is

A done-for-you (then done-with-you) setup that connects a business owner's email, calendar, documents, and key business systems into a single AI operating layer on Codex, grounded in how they actually run their business — so the system can act on their behalf and compound over time.

It replaces the function of a $2,000+/mo online business manager, and points toward standing up CFO/COO-grade business agents.

## Pricing & Packaging

| Component | Price | What It Covers |
|---|---|---|
| **System Integration** | **$500 flat** | Connect email, calendar, documents + key business systems; set up the Codex harness; load grounding context; stand up first agents/automations; one booked next step. |
| **Retainer** | **$85 / hour** | Ongoing work: new agents, data analysis, campaign management, automations, troubleshooting. |
| **Hour blocks** (option) | 10 hrs prepaid | For owners who want runway and priority; smooths the relationship. |
| **Project scope** (option) | Quoted in hours | A specific deliverable — e.g. drop pre-work, a CFO-agent buildout, a content migration. |

Comparison anchor to use in conversation: **$200/mo** for the Codex Business plan vs. **$2,000+/mo** for an online business manager the system replaces.

## What's In Scope (the $500 setup)

- **Structure first:** a lean business canvas / problem statement so AI has grounding before it's connected.
- **Data connection:** email (Gmail), calendar, documents (Drive), and the key business systems (e.g. Shopify, Mercury, QuickBooks where relevant).
- **Grounding context:** differentiated-messaging doc + ROI-drivers doc + lean BMC loaded as core context files.
- **First agents/automations:** at least one follow-up-draft flow, one scheduled BI/monitoring report, and any "always-on" jobs (content migration, newsletter, opportunity scanner).
- **Harness + seats:** Codex Business plan setup, multi-seat so a teammate/intern can take hand-off jobs; run on an always-on machine (e.g. an office Mac mini) with tunnel access.
- **A booked next step.**

## What's Out Of Scope (until retainer / project)

- Deep CFO-agent buildout (needs statement-of-cash-flows analysis, waterfall, P&L + balance-sheet reconciliation — heavy grounding).
- Bank-account *write* access / money movement (guardrails + human approval required; start read-only).
- Custom plugins / MCP servers (e.g. QuickBooks MCP ~2 hrs) — quoted as project work.
- Reactive production dashboards / BI products (the setup gives read surfaces for *chatting with data*, not a BI app).
- Anything requiring third-party plugin code review and hardening.

## Standard Deliverables

1. Connected data sources (email, calendar, docs + business systems).
2. Three grounding docs: differentiated messaging, ROI drivers, lean BMC.
3. One working follow-up/draft flow.
4. One scheduled report + monitoring.
5. Any always-on jobs running (content, newsletter, scanner).
6. Multi-seat harness on an always-on node.
7. A short "how to steer it" handoff + a booked next step.

## Honest Expectations (set these every time)

- **Babysitting is real.** Permissions prompts, version drift (the iPhone↔Mac break in the Imtiaz session), and ~30% disposable output. The human value is curation and steering.
- **It's chatting with your data, not a dashboard product.**
- **Guardrails before bank access.** Read-only first; human approval before money moves.
- **Grounding is the work.** The integration is easy; aligning the system to how the owner actually runs the business is the part that takes humanity.

## Why It Compounds (the upsell logic)

Every skill, context doc, and automation built makes the *whole organization* faster — and transfers to the next teammate on a new seat. One setup → recurring retainer → more agents → more seats. The $500 is the wedge; the compounding is the business.
