Doctrine

What you never pay Zoth for

Keys

You bring them. The vault is yours. Plaintext never leaves this machine, and we never hold a KMS over you.

Inference

Local Ollama or your own APIs. Zoth does not meter tokens, sell credits, or rent you a hosted model.

Telemetry

None. Zero usage tracking, zero phone-home. You host the workspace. We do not watch the deck.

Answers

Do I have to pay to use Zoth?

No. The operator OS is $0. Install, deck, vault, swarm, agents and BYOK all run on your machine, and you host the workspace. If anyone ever asks you to pay for a seat, that is not this product.

This product is Zoth Studio. NullAI (nullai.tech) is paid professional services: SLA, hardware, and custom foundry. It is not a SaaS seat and it does not lock the vault.
